## What have I been working on?
I'm still working on my ballcatcher bot, I've finally been able to get two virtual cameras to work and recognize a virtual ball. I also got homing working on the physical bot so it essentially acts like a 3D printer. Granted I needed to scope back, the bot is almost done and my hard deadline is next week.

What really sucked about the ballcatcher bot is I had to redesign the Fusion 360 model from the ground up after finding out the core rotational aspect of the bot would be a huge issue (namely my slip rings didn't have the correct power delivery for USB and was having issues with power delivery). I ended up making the bot work on a limited degree of 270 for the pitch, similar to the way a 3D printer works.

While I did run into the challenge of needing to redesign the bot from the ground up, I thought it was a good learning experience to know what design considerations are for physical hardware. I learned I should always think about the core important mechanics of bot and test the fundamentals before going ahead with my assumptions.

Other than my challenges, I did well in considering how the yaw and pitch of the bot would actually work. It's interesting to understand what steps in a stepper motor actually mean and one intuitive small thing I could think about is realizing that gear ratios are simply the ratio of the motor to the stick controlling (in my case, the motor was connected at a 1/3 gear ratio to the stick)
